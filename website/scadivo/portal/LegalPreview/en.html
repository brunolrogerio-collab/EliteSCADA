This notice explains how we use personal data on the SCADIVO website, Portal, and Community.

Account and profile

When you sign up, we use the date of birth you provide only to check whether you are at least 16 years old. We do not save that date in your profile.

We use your email address, username, display name, and language to manage your account and send confirmation, recovery, and service messages. Profile type, field of work or study, and employer or educational institution are optional and help us produce internal metrics. Your phone number, address, and date of birth are also optional in your profile. You can change your marketing preference in the Portal.

Support, licenses, and downloads

We use the information and messages you provide to respond to inquiries and support tickets. To manage licenses, we use the link to the account, organization, and application and, depending on the license type, an installation or device identifier. We record when downloads start for each item to help manage the catalog.

Community

When you sign in with a SCADIVO account, the Community receives your account ID, verified email address, username, display name, and language. Who can see a post depends on the category permissions. When an account is closed, the team anonymizes the public attribution of its posts. If a post contains personal data in its text, you can ask us to review that content.

Cookies, security, and hosting

The Portal and Community use cookies to maintain your session. The Portal also uses them to protect forms. The website and Portal may save your language preference in your browser. Website and Portal visit counts use daily totals without individual identifiers in those counts. SCADIVO infrastructure is hosted by HostGator in Brazil. We use email services to deliver service messages and encrypted backups for recovery.

Retention

Eligible unconfirmed registrations are deleted after 30 days. Closed tickets and inquiries are kept for 12 months after closure. Final email delivery records are kept for 90 days. After 12 months of inactivity in the Portal, eligible accounts may receive a notice before the team reviews them.

Access logs are kept for six months from the date of access, subject to confirmation of the applicable legal requirements. Administrative and security audit events are kept for 12 months from the event date. We separately retain the minimum records needed to meet retention obligations and prevent duplicate inactivity notices.

Daily totals without individual identifiers are kept for 24 months. We keep the minimum record of completed requests and ended licenses for 24 months after closure for support and disputes. We preserve the data needed for valid licenses. Tax record retention will be defined when commercial purchasing is available.

Daily backups on the server are kept for 14 days. Off-site and ad hoc backups are kept for 30 days from creation, with documented exceptions. After recovery, we reapply deletions, corrections, and access blocks made since the backup. We preserve records subject to a retention obligation.

Your rights

Go to Contact and select Privacy to request access to or a copy of your data, corrections, information about data sharing, account closure or, where applicable, deletion, anonymization, blocking, portability, or withdrawal of consent. We may verify your identity to protect your account.

We will announce significant changes in the Portal and by email, with a summary, the effective date, and access to the updated text.

Eligibility

The pilot is intended for technical users aged 16 and over. The Portal provides an account, support, Community access, and license management.

Using your account

Keep your contact information up to date and protect your credentials. When acting for an organization, follow the permissions it has granted you. Contact support if you suspect someone is using your account without permission.

Licenses

Your issued license specifies the license type, features, limits, and validity. Online licenses may require periodic renewal. Offline, device-bound licenses follow the terms of issuance and remain tied to the specified device.

Community

Follow the Community rules. Post only material you have the right to share and keep personal and confidential information out of public topics. Test technical guidance in a controlled environment before applying it to real installations. You can ask support to review a moderation or suspension decision.

Closing your account

Request closure under Contact → Privacy. After we verify your identity, closing your account blocks sign-in and new activations. Valid licenses are preserved. Existing online installations can continue renewing while both the license and activation remain valid. Contact support if you need to recover an activation or change devices after closing your account.

Support and changes to these terms

Open a support ticket in the Portal or use the website’s Contact area. We will announce significant changes in the Portal and by email, with a summary, the effective date, and a link to the new version.
