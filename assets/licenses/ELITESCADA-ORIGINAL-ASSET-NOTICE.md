# EliteSCADA original/static asset provenance notice

`Proprietary-EliteSCADA-Original` is an internal catalog identifier used for artwork authored specifically for the EliteSCADA product and not imported from a third-party artwork library.

It is **not** a public license grant. It records that the candidate is intended to be product-owned/original content for which commercial redistribution and modification are permitted by the project owner.

For `generated/original` artwork, this notice alone does not approve distribution. The Asset Factory requires explicit review evidence before an item can reach `approved`.

Third-party assets must use their actual license identifier/reference and must separately record whether commercial redistribution, modification and attribution are permitted. Unknown or ambiguous terms must remain non-approved.
