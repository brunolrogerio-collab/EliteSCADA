## 1. Initial asset/process scope

Prioritize **motor-driven pumping sets**, covering condition and process performance under an explicitly configured operating context. Use **motor-driven air compressors** as the second research/validation case because MetroPT provides real operating variability. A public benchmark qualifies only its demonstrated cases; it does not establish compatibility or accuracy for every pump, motor or compressor.

The first questions are: has the electrical demand changed for a comparable declared process demand, and have pressure/flow relationships changed under the selected context? Include motor current or measured active power, flow and available pressure signals; temperature is optional. Speed/frequency and running/load states are context inputs when available. Vibration is optional and high-frequency waveform analysis remains outside this package. Use 2–8 numeric target signals through the new additive contract; do not change the frozen 4A–4E behavior.

Use the existing versioned Stage 4E predicates and stable TAG identities. Configure comparison windows for the same declared profile/version; exclude startup, shutdown, setpoint changes or other phases only through explicit supported context predicates. If the data cannot distinguish those phases, report the eligibility limitation. Equal speed alone does not establish equal load or global regime equivalence.

Reuse the existing KPI catalog for supported performance calculations. Current is useful as a load/condition signal; electrical efficiency or energy per output requires the appropriate measured power/output. A pump efficiency claim requires suitable hydraulic and input-power evidence. Do not turn amperes into an inferred efficiency percentage. This follows the system-level measurement approach in the [DOE pumping sourcebook](https://www.energy.gov/sites/default/files/2014/05/f16/pump.pdf). The [ISO 17359 public description](https://www.iso.org/standard/71194.html) supplies the general condition-monitoring framework; no paid normative clauses have been used to invent numerical requirements.

## 2. Operating objective and evaluation units

The default is **conservative advisory monitoring** for sustained deviations from the selected reference. No process command, alarm-engine occurrence, maintenance instruction or automatic protection is added by this acceptance plan.

Evaluate both individual eligible cohort decisions and sustained episodes. For the offline acceptance harness, a candidate episode is confirmed when at least **3 of 5 consecutive eligible decisions** exceed the fixed candidate threshold, within a period of at most **5 minutes**. The five decisions must come from distinct, complete source-time cohorts at the declared cadence. An ineligible decision, missing expected cohort, context change or a gap greater than twice the declared cadence breaks the run; it does not count as a normal vote. If cadence is unknown, this temporal episode metric is unavailable. Do not resample, interpolate or create synchronization to obtain five decisions.

An episode ends after **5 consecutive eligible below-threshold decisions**; missing/ineligible data interrupts the episode as unknown availability. A later confirmation is a new episode, including after an interruption, so missing data cannot hide repeated notifications. This rule is an evaluation convention only: operational notification/state persistence would require a separate scoped implementation. It does not create background analysis or alter the on-demand architecture.

Measure latency from labeled deviation onset to the first confirmed episode, with unconfirmed/missed cases retained as misses. Count one detection per labeled episode and report overlap and false episodes. Do not credit an entire fault range merely because one late sample was detected. Event-based and point-based results answer different questions, as discussed by [Tatbul et al., Precision and Recall for Time Series](https://papers.nips.cc/paper_files/paper/2018/hash/8f468c873a32bb0619eaeb2050ba45d1-Abstract.html).

## 3. Initial quantitative acceptance targets

| Criterion | Main's initial target | Required evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Point false positives | At most **1%** of eligible, independently labeled reference/known-nonfault held-out cohort decisions | Report numerator/denominator per profile/context and time block; excluded/missing decisions reported separately |
| False advisory episodes | At most **1 per 24 eligible operating hours**, per asset/profile/context | Use the fixed episode convention; report operating exposure and observed count, not calendar duration with inactive periods |
| Detection of defined sustained deviations | At least **90%** episode recall in each required injection class; at least 90% of all injected episodes detected within **10 minutes** for declared cadence at most 10 seconds | At least 100 distinct eligible held-out source blocks per required class; missed/late cases fail the relevant proportion. Slower cadence requires its own measured latency qualification |
| Required injected classes | Single-target level shift, multi-target level shift, and cross-signal relationship change | Level shifts of at least **3 frozen reference robust-scale units**, maintained at least **15 minutes**; relationship-change effect specified independently of candidate score before testing |
| Smaller/gradual changes | Characterize **1- and 2-scale-unit** shifts and slow ramps; report recall/latency | Exploratory measurements, with no promised sensitivity or fault-prediction claim |
| Data/security boundaries | **100%** of defined invalid-data, context, authorization, disabled-scope and query-budget cases fail closed as specified | No score on ineligible data, no unauthorized read and no work for disabled scopes; appropriate local/CI evidence on Windows and Linux |

Each strong-shift scenario must preserve the declared context and other data-quality conditions. Robust scale is computed only from the fitting reference with a documented, versioned definition and units; it is a test-effect convention, not the final detector threshold. Zero/degenerate scale makes that scenario ineligible, not a license to divide by an epsilon. For the relationship-change class, specify a physically plausible, context-preserving perturbation or measured labeled event that changes a documented relation; freeze its magnitude in raw units/reference residual units before testing. Do not define the stimulus by exceeding the candidate score's own threshold.

Known non-fault load changes within the configured context must be tested against the false-positive targets; transitions outside that context must produce non-comparable/insufficient state rather than being counted as successful fault detections. Maintenance-report windows and natural degradation are reported separately from injected events. Absence of a maintenance report is not, by itself, a verified healthy label.

Use at least **168 eligible normal/known-nonfault operating hours across 14 calendar days** before judging the operating-hour false-episode target. A shorter bench dataset may support method/contract evidence but cannot pass that exposure claim. Report 95% uncertainty intervals and variation by independent day/cycle group for false-positive rate, episode rate, recall and latency. With dependence, use justified block/cluster uncertainty; a binomial interval over adjacent rows is insufficient. If usable independent groups or coverage are inadequate, mark the affected metric INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE even if its point estimate looks good. These exposure and episode counts are validation-plan floors, not the unselected covariance-fitting sample-size rule.

The [NIST control-chart discussion](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/pmc/section3/pmc32.htm) explains the sensitivity/false-alarm tradeoff and dependence on assumptions. A nominal calibration quantile is not a demonstrated operating false-positive rate. Main selected the above conservative advisory targets for this first scope; the normal-distribution three-sigma example is not being transplanted as a guaranteed multivariate threshold.

## 4. Method study and leakage controls

Study robust per-signal centering/scaling and a regularized covariance joint distance as the ADR-021 candidate; compare it to the frozen independent-vector result as a baseline. Preserve cross-signal information: a diagonal score relabeled as joint is not sufficient. [NIST's Hotelling discussion](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/pmc/section3/pmc341.htm) motivates a covariance-aware distance, and [Ledoit and Wolf](https://www.ledoit.net/Well-conditioned2004.pdf) motivates evaluating conditioning/shrinkage rather than relying on unstable inverse covariance. Neither source fixes the estimator or guarantees its calibration for these industrial time series.

Default study split: **60% fitting reference, 20% calibration, 20% final holdout**, in chronological whole-run/day/cycle blocks. Keep a whole event in one partition; keep injected counterparts with their source block. Publish the manifest before fitting. If that split omits a required context or event class, design and record a suitable blocked split before fitting instead of shuffling adjacent rows across partitions. Purge overlapping windows and use a gap at least as long as the feature/decision lookback, extended where measured dependence requires it. Source rates/gaps must be measured first; [NIST on autocorrelation](https://itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/eda/section3/eda35c.htm) explains why sequential observations cannot automatically be treated as independent.

Fit scaling/covariance on fitting data only. Select any candidate configuration and threshold using calibration data only, including separately declared calibration perturbations if used. Freeze them before touching the final holdout; do not retune to the holdout or use failure labels to select a favorable threshold afterward. A failed final test requires a documented new experiment and fresh holdout; Main must not silently relax targets to produce PASS. Record seeds, manifests, hashes, units, contexts and exact software version.

Public data with unknown quality/source-time evidence may support an explicitly labeled offline method study. It must not be promoted to `Good`, `Synchronized`, a source/server timestamp or canonical capture-policy provenance. A same-row timestamp or cycle index is not proof of the canonical synchronization requirement. The end-to-end product contract and field-validity claims each need their own adequate evidence. Main's acceptance targets do not waive this requirement.

## 5. Data-use and retention decision

Start with the two official UCI datasets in #659, subject to verification of their CC BY 4.0 notices and any embedded terms: [MetroPT-3](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/791/metropt%2B3%2Bdataset) and [hydraulic-system condition monitoring](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/447/condition%2Bmonitoring%2Bof%2Bhydraulic%2Bsystems). Preserve authors, DOI, download URL, retrieval date, immutable file hashes and an account of transformations. This authorization is for the bounded research/evaluation task, not for redistributing raw data inside the product.

Keep downloaded raw data and intermediate subsets in isolated local research storage, outside Git/GitHub, for at most **90 days from retrieval**. Schedule a documented retention review before expiry; any extension must record purpose and a new expiry. Delete expendable intermediate copies within **30 days after the research task closes**, preserving the pinned source manifest and compact evaluation results as repository evidence. Record exact paths and actual deletion disposition; this document does not itself delete any file. Reproducibility relies on pinned public sources, scripts and manifests rather than indefinite duplicate raw copies.

No private-plant export is required merely to begin this public-data study. If these sources prove insufficient, Main will state the exact missing signals/provenance/scenarios before requesting a specific authorized export. Do not collect third-party or owner operational data implicitly. This research policy does not alter production Historian retention, stable Preview data or existing customer data-use permissions.
